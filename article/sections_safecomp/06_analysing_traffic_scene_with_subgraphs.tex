\section{Coverage Gap Analysis Using Subgraphs}
\label{chapter:analysing_traffic_scene_with_subgraphs}



This chapter identifies coverage gaps---scenarios present in Argoverse but absent or underrepresented in CARLA---across three complementary dimensions, comparing a \textit{TEST} dataset against a \textit{REF} dataset: (1)~\textbf{structural coverage}, the presence or absence of scenario archetypes in graph topology; (2)~\textbf{parametric distributions}, differences in continuous node and edge attributes (e.g.\ speeds, path lengths) within the same archetype; and (3)~\textbf{co-occurrence patterns}, which combinations of archetypes appear together in a scene. These are applied to CARLA and Argoverse in Section~\ref{chapter:application}.
